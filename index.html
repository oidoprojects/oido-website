WE MAKE
THINGS
THAT STAY.

Oído Projects is a stainless steel and aluminum fabrication studio serving the hospitality industry in Southern California. We design and manufacture custom pieces — from front-of-house statement objects to full back-of-house kitchen systems.

We operate at the intersection of industrial craft and architectural design. Every project begins with a conversation and ends with something built to outlast the space it lives in.

The name Oído is the Spanish word for ear. In professional kitchens, when a chef calls an order, cooks respond — Oído. Heard. Understood. On it.

"Always custom. Never predefined."
